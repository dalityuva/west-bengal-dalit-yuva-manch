ABOUT US

Creating Opportunities. Inspiring Change.

West Bengal Dalit Yuva Manch is a youth-focused community initiative working toward education, empowerment, awareness and inclusive development.

We believe that young people are an important part of the present as well as the future. By creating opportunities for learning, leadership, sports, creativity and skill development, we aim to help communities become stronger.
